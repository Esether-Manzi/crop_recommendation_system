How do I fill this in?

We don't measure your soil ourselves. Enter the results of a soil test you already have from a soil testing laboratory or a soil test kit (your district agricultural or extension officer can tell you where to get one). Weather values are averages for your area.

  • Nitrogen (N), mg/kg: the available (mineral) nitrogen on your report. If your report only shows Total N in %, that is a different measurement and can't be converted. Ask the lab or your extension officer for available N.
  • Phosphorus (P), mg/kg: the available P (often called Bray or Mehlich P). ppm is the same as mg/kg.
  • Potassium (K), mg/kg: if your report gives cmol/kg or meq/100 g, use the converter below.
  • Soil pH: copy it as shown. If there are two, use pH (water).
  • Temperature (°C) and humidity (%): the yearly average for your area, from district weather records, the Uganda National Meteorological Authority, or your extension officer.
  • Rainfall, mm per year: the total rain in a normal year. If you only know the monthly average, multiply it by 12.
